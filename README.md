Welcome to this repository! 
In August 2025, Riffusion officially rebranded as **Producer.ai**, introducing its new AI music model, **Fuzz 2.0** (including Fuzz 2.0 Raw).

This repository serves as a living resource for exploring templates, prompts, instructions, and workflows for the Fuzz 2.0 model. It's a practical guide based on my experience as a paid member (Starter) using new features like chat mode, custom flows, and custom instructions.

### What You'll Find Here
* **Templates & Prompts:** A collection of tested and ready-to-use prompts for various music genres and styles.
* **Workflows:** Step-by-step workflows and instructions to generate consistent, high-quality outputs.
* **Tips & Tricks:** Various tips and tricks to get the most out of chat mode and custom instructions.
